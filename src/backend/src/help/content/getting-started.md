Nucleus is where your organisation describes its data: what data exists, who owns it,
what it promises to deliver, and who may use it. You do not need to know Databricks to
use it. This page covers your first few minutes.

## What you see when you first sign in

You land on **Home**. What Home shows depends on your role:

- **You have a role.** Home shows an **Overview**, **My Actions** (approvals waiting for
  you and **Quick Actions**), sections chosen for your role such as **Discovery** or
  **Data Curation**, and **Recent Activity**.
- **You have no role yet.** Home shows a **Roles** section with **Available Roles** and a
  **Request Access** button on each one. If no roles are listed, Home asks you to contact
  an administrator.

To check your role, open your user menu (your name, top right). It shows **Role:**
followed by your current role.

If you have no role, start with [Requesting access](/help/requesting-access).

## Finding your way around

- **The side menu** groups the pages. **Discover** has **Marketplace** and **Data Catalog**.
  **Build** has **Assets**, **Concepts**, **Data Contracts** and **Products → All
  products**. Further groups (**Organization**, **Semantics**, **Workflows**, **Settings**)
  appear only if your role can use them.
- **Preview features.** Some areas are marked beta or alpha. They appear only when you turn
  them on under **Feature Previews** in your user menu (**Show Beta Features**, **Show Alpha
  Features**).
- **The search box** in the top bar searches assets, concepts, contracts, products and
  these help articles.
- **The bell** in the top bar holds your notifications: requests you sent, decisions on
  them, and approvals waiting for you.

You only see menu items your role can open. If a colleague mentions a page you cannot
find, your role probably does not include it. See
[Roles and permissions](/help/roles-and-permissions).
